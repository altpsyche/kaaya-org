Blooming explores the connection between human emotions and nature. Just as a flower spreads its fragrance when it blooms, a joyful person naturally shares happiness, warmth, and positivity with those around them. In this painting, I combine the form of a tree with a feminine figure whose face blossoms into flowers, symbolizing growth, love, and renewal. Nature, often called Mother Nature, gives selflessly and nurtures all life. Similarly, feminine energy represents unconditional love, compassion, and the ability to nurture. This energy is not defined by gender—it exists in everyone.
