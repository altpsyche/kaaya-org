“There’s a fox that lies within you, always hidden. I see it in the way you smile when you think nobody’s watching. Have you ever noticed?”

This work explores the hidden instincts that exist within every human. The fox is a metaphor for the untamed self ,our desires, fears, impulses, and emotions that often remain concealed beneath a civilised identity. Its distorted, absurd form reflects the complexity of human nature, while the faded text and trapped text work in cage symbolise suppressed thoughts and instincts shaped by society. The work invites viewers to recognise and question the hidden self that quietly exists within us all.
