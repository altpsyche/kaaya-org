Nisha Chauhan is a young painter from Bageshwar, Uttarakhand, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Fine Arts at Soban Singh Jeena University, Almora. Working across acrylic, watercolour, poster colour, graphite, and mixed media, her practice explores the relationship between the human figure and the raw, elemental force of nature. Deeply influenced by the silence and scale of the Himalayan landscape, she investigates themes of beauty, fear, fragility, and human instinct through figurative and landscape-based compositions. Ancient sculpture and classical forms strongly shape her visual language, inspiring her understanding of the body as both sacred and vulnerable. Currently in an active phase of experimentation, Nisha continues to explore new ideas, mediums, and subjects while searching for her evolving artistic voice.
