Vijay Vikram Singh is a visual artist from Rudrapur, Uttarakhand, whose practice explores psychological transformation, memory, and self-reflection through symbolic and layered visual language. Holding a BFA in Painting and Printmaking from Jammu University and an MFA in Painting from Noida International University, he works primarily with acrylic and mixed media to create introspective compositions rooted in the concept of Metanoia — inner rupture and renewal. Influenced by the Himalayan landscape, he transforms natural forms such as roots, leaves, and organic growth into metaphors for emotional states, uncertainty, and emergence. His layered works exist between fragmentation and healing, inviting viewers into moments of pause, reflection, and personal transformation where inner and outer worlds quietly intersect.
