Hailing from the high-altitude deserts of Mustang, Tenzin Norbu captures the spiritual and ecological essence of the Himalayan landscape. His work blends traditional thangka techniques with modern conceptual art, creating a bridge between ancestral wisdom and contemporary dialogue. Norbu's paintings are characterized by their deep azure pigments and intricate golden line work, reflecting the celestial clarity found only in the peaks.
