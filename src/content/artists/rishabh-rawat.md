Rishabh Rawat is a visual artist from Uttarakhand, currently based in Dehradun. A graduate of the University of Hyderabad in Printmaking and Expanded Media, his practice explores emotional vulnerability, inner conflict, and suppressed psychological states through printmaking, text, and figurative imagery.

Through absurd, ambiguous, and deeply personal compositions, Rishabh transforms private emotional experiences into shared spaces of reflection, confronting themes of repression, aggression, fragility, and emotional release with honesty and sensitivity.
